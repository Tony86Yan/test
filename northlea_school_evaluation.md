# Northlea Elementary and Middle School - Comprehensive Evaluation

**Evaluation Date:** November 10, 2025  
**School Type:** Public Elementary and Middle School  
**Location:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada (Leaside neighborhood)

## Executive Summary

Northlea Elementary and Middle School (JK-8) is a well-regarded public school in Toronto's Leaside neighborhood, serving students from Junior Kindergarten through Grade 8. This evaluation examines the school's performance across multiple dimensions including academic achievement, programs, facilities, and community engagement.

## Overall Assessment

### Summary
Northlea Elementary and Middle School is a high-quality public school that provides a well-rounded education in a supportive and inclusive environment. The school excels in:

✅ **Academic Excellence**: Strong performance on standardized assessments  
✅ **Comprehensive Programs**: Wide range of curricular and extracurricular offerings

✅ **Inclusive Environment**: Commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion  
✅ **Location**: Excellent neighborhood with strong community support  

### Considerations for Prospective Families

**Best Fit For:**
- Families seeking a well-rounded public school education
- Those valuing community involvement and parent engagement
- Students who thrive in inclusive, diverse environments
- Families prioritizing both academics and character development

**Things to Consider:**
- Popularity may mean waitlists for out-of-catchment students
- Class sizes reflect typical Toronto public school ratios
- JK-8 structure means transition to high school after Grade 8
